A premium dark, local-first comic and manga reader for Windows and Android, with a smooth reader and a Drift-backed library.

⬇ Download for Windows ⬇ Download for Android (APK)

CBZ, ZIP & PDF

Import chapters with natural page ordering. PDFs open in a PDFium-powered viewer.

Flexible reader

Page mode or continuous scroll, rotation, fit-screen or fit-width, zoom and keyboard navigation.

Remembers where you stopped

Page and scroll position are restored, with history, favourites and reading stats.

Local-first

Your library lives in a local SQLite database on your device.

CBR and CB7 formats are not supported yet. On Windows, extract the ZIP and run the .exe inside. On Android, allow installing from unknown sources to sideload the APK.
